## Feedback Hardening Effectiveness

The updated checklist from `docs/review/improvements/v0.2_feedback_hardening.md` measurably changed Batch 002 behavior:

1. **Provenance gating is working:** blocked-source rejections are now explicit and early.
2. **Depth rubric is working:** hardware/business records are routed to `needs_revision` instead of ambiguous approvals.
3. **Conditional approvals are working:** RAIL-M and research-only records are accepted with explicit retention requirements rather than hidden behind generic approvals.
4. **Avoidable issues reduced:** the 20% rejection rate from Batch 001 dropped to 4%, indicating fewer records are being pulled into review without cleared provenance.

---

## Common Issues

1. Provenance/attribution gaps remain the highest-risk upstream failure mode for conditional-license sources.
2. Thin canonical answers in hardware/business categories still need operational context to meet training-usefulness standards.
3. License labels such as `(gated; verify)` and `(must human-review)` require explicit documentation retention even when the underlying content is approved.

---

## Recommendations

1. Close the attribution-tracking workflow for `needs_revision` StackExchange/CC-BY-SA records before the next batch.
2. Complete PII-policy and LMSYS usage-note reviews for conditionally approved records before release.
3. Keep curated files immutable during review; all review artifacts stay under `review/` and `docs/`.
4. Do not release v0.2 until all 150 expansion records are human-reviewed and gate state is updated.
5. Continue using `quality_score=7 + lower confidence + category diversity` ordering for subsequent batches.
